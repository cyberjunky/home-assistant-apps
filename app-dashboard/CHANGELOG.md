# Changelog

## 0.1.1

**After updating:** nothing to do.

- **Search to add:** a block's settings have a search for entities to add to it. It shows only what that block takes; click one, or press Enter for the first, to add it. Dragging still works.
- The screenshots show the white background.

## 0.1.0

First version.

- **Pages you make yourself:** press the pencil and drag your entities from the drawer onto a page. An entity dropped on an empty spot becomes its own block, on a block that takes more it joins it, and on the top bar it becomes a reading that shows on every page. Move and size blocks on a grid that always fills the screen.
- **Blocks:** clock, heading, value, weather with the days or hours ahead, tiles, slider, climate, blinds, actions, list, media with the album cover, bars, split bar, chart (a line, or a bar per day), agenda and camera.
- **Popups:** tap a reading for its history (24 hours, 7 or 30 days, with lowest, highest and average, or totals per day for energy), hold a light, blind or speaker for its slider, tap the weather for the forecast and a camera for its live picture.
- **Doorbell:** when your doorbell goes, every tablet shows the camera you chose.
- **Time of day:** a block can show only in the morning, during the day, in the evening or at night. At night the tablet can turn dark, dim, and show one page; a touch wakes it. Left alone, it can go back to its first page.
- **Look:** six bundled fonts, the weight of the large numbers, both backgrounds and the accent colour; a block can have its own colour and a panel behind it.
- **Keeping it:** the dashboard is saved in the app's data, is part of Home Assistant's backups, and can be saved as a file and loaded again.
