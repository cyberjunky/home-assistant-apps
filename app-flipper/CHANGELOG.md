## 0.1.0

First version.

- **Device:** the Flipper's screen, live, and its buttons on the page and the keyboard (short and long presses); name, firmware, battery and SD card; set the clock, find it, restart it; save a screenshot.
- **Code:** projects with an editor. C apps are built with ufbt and started on the Flipper with **Build & run**; JavaScript is put on the Flipper and run. Three templates, import and export as zip.
- **Files:** browse, upload, download, rename, delete and run what is on the SD card and the internal storage.
- **Console:** the Flipper's command line and its log.
- The Flipper SDK and compiler are downloaded on request, kept in the app's data and left out of backups.
