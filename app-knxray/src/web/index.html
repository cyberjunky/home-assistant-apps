By handan interface the search does not find

Without an interface of its own the app listens along with Home Assistant: the monitor, the recording, the charts and every check of the project and the entities work that way, and nothing has to be chosen here.

An interface of its own adds what cannot be done through Home Assistant: asking a device directly whether it is there and what it is, scanning a line, and finding devices in programming mode. Routing takes no tunnel; tunnelling takes one of the interface's tunnels (most have four or five), so one stays free for ETS.

The connection only reads: it sends reads and the handshake of a connection to a device, never a write, a restart or a download. KNX Secure interfaces are not supported; use Home Assistant's connection for those.

Interfaces on the network

What this is

An X-ray of a KNX installation: it listens to the bus, holds what it hears against the ETS project and against what Home Assistant has configured for KNX, and says what does not add up. It only reads. KNX-Ray is an unofficial KNX application: it is not made by, or associated with, the KNX Association.

Where the data comes from

  • The ETS project (names, datapoint types, devices, their objects and flags) is the one uploaded in Home Assistant's KNX settings. Upload a fresh export after changes in ETS: the app follows.
  • Telegrams come from the app's own KNX interface when one is chosen on the Interface page, and otherwise from Home Assistant's KNX integration.
  • Home Assistant's entities, made in the KNX panel or written in YAML, come from the KNX integration.

Reading the findings

Errors are things that do not work. Warnings are things that will bite: a read nobody answers, an address the project does not know. Notes are leftovers and things to know. Every kind says what to do about it. A finding you have judged and want out of sight can be hidden; it stays under Hidden.

What is said about the bus is about what was seen since the app started. Checks of the project and of Home Assistant are always complete.

The recording

Every telegram is kept in the app's data, for as long as the options say (90 days and two million telegrams unless changed). The Archive, the Charts and the Statistics read from it. It is part of Home Assistant's backups.

Good to know

  • A group address without a datapoint type cannot be decoded. Its payload is shown as it is, and a chart reads it by its length.
  • Values of KNX Data Secure addresses are only readable through Home Assistant's connection, which has the keys.
  • Wire on the Monitor page is bus monitor mode: every frame on the line with its acknowledgement. Some interfaces close their other tunnels while it is on.
  • Stray entities can be removed from the Home Assistant page. Switch “Allow removing” off in the app's options to make the app only report.
