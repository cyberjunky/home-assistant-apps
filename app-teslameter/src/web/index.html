Fetch charging history adds past Supercharge sessions from Tesla's records (energy, place and billed cost). The backup holds all recorded data; your Tesla sign-in is stored separately and is never part of a download.

Danger zone

Deletes every recorded trip, charging session, position, state and software version. Your cars, your Tesla sign-in and your charging prices stay, so logging simply continues. Download a backup first; this cannot be undone.

Charging prices

Tag where you charge and give each tag a price per kWh. A charging session is priced by its location's tag; untagged locations use the default price from the add-on configuration, and Superchargers keep the amount Tesla billed. Assign a location's tag on any session in the Charging view.
