Before you add games

This app contains the MAME emulator and no games. A game is a ROM file: a copy of the program chips of the original arcade machine. Those are copyrighted by the companies that made the games, also when they are decades old and no longer sold.

Only add ROM files you have the right to use: dumped from an arcade board you own, bought as a licensed release, or released for free by the rights holder. The MAME team offers a set of such free ROMs on mamedev.org/roms. Downloading other ROMs from download sites is not legal in most countries, the Netherlands included. You are responsible for the files you add.
