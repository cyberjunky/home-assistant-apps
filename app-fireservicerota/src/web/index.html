Where the data comes from

Everything on this dashboard is read from Home Assistant's FireServiceRota integration: its incidents sensor, duty binary sensor and incident response switch, live over Home Assistant's websocket and from its recorder history. This app never contacts FireServiceRota or Brandweerrooster itself. Acknowledge and Reject switch the integration's response switch, which sends your answer. Only the message player (▶) lets your browser fetch the spoken message from FireServiceRota, when you press play.

Stored data
The app keeps every incident, its updates, your responses and your duty changes in its own database, also after Home Assistant's recorder has purged them. The app's options (entities, history, events, announcements) are on its Configuration tab in Home Assistant.

History and backup
Import from Home Assistant
Reads the three entities' history from Home Assistant's recorder again (as far back as history_days, if the recorder still has it). Nothing is stored twice.
-
Backup and restore
Download the whole database as one file, or restore such a file, for example on new hardware. Home Assistant backups include the database too.
Danger zone
Reset deletes everything in the app's database: incidents, updates, responses and duty. Home Assistant's own history is not touched; Import history reads back what the recorder still has. Download a backup first.
